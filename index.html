Are these downloads free?
Yes! Zentry is committed to open-source and free software. Everything listed here is free to download.
How do I submit an app?
Click the "Submit App" button in the navigation bar to see the submission guidelines. We welcome community contributions!

Submit Your App

We love community contributions! To add your software or game to Zentry, follow these steps:

  1. Fork the Zentry GitHub Repository.
  2. Upload your project files to the /downloads folder.
  3. Edit index.html and add your item to the catalog list.
  4. Submit a Pull Request (PR) for review.

💡 Tip: Make sure to include a clear description, version number, and at least one tag.

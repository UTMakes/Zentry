How do I submit an app?
Click the "Submit App" button in the navigation bar to see the submission guidelines. We welcome community contributions!

Secure Submission

To ensure the safety of our users, we manually review every submission. Please follow this secure process:

  1. Upload your project/game to a cloud host (Google Drive, Dropbox, or GitHub Releases).
  2. Ensure the file is publicly accessible via link.
  3. Fill out the form below with your App Details and Download Link.
  4. Our team will scan the file and add it to the catalog within 24 hours.

Security Note: We do not accept direct file uploads or PRs from unverified accounts to prevent malware.
